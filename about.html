About ToolPick Review and how we make money

ToolPick Review is an independent review site. We write about software people actually search for and try to answer one question honestly: which tool should you install, and what are its weak spots.

How the site is funded

Through affiliate links. When a reader follows a link to a vendor and buys a product, the vendor pays us a commission. The price you pay does not change, and no vendor can pay for a better review or a higher ranking. Every page with affiliate links carries a disclosure at the bottom.

Our principles

  • Trade-offs are stated, not hidden.
  • If a free tool solves the task, we say so instead of pushing a paid one.
  • Prices, plans and features change — check the vendor's page before buying.
  • We do not publish sponsored reviews.
