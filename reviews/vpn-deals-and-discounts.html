VPN deals and discounts: how to pay less without getting burned

VPN pricing is designed for one thing: to make the first year cheap and the second year expensive. Here is how to buy well.

The four rules

  • Never renew at list price. Cancelling and re-subscribing on a new promotion is usually cheaper than renewing — check the terms first.
  • Longer plans only after testing. Two-year deals lock you in before you know whether the VPN works with your streaming services.
  • Use the refund window as a free trial — most reputable providers offer 30 days.
  • Ignore "lifetime" claims from unknown vendors. Servers cost money every month; someone has to pay for them.

Are free VPNs worth it? Only from vendors whose business model is paid plans.
