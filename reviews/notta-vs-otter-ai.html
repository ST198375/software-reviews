Both turn speech into text. They differ in what happens after the transcript appears — and that is where your time is saved or lost.

How to decide

  • You record first and transcribe later → Notta.
  • You live in Zoom or Meet and want notes in real time → Otter.
  • You need subtitles for video → Notta's SRT/VTT export is the shorter path.

Our pick for most people: Notta — handles existing recordings, several speakers and exports ready for documents or video subtitles.
