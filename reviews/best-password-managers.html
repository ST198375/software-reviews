Best password managers: the cheapest security upgrade you can make

Reusing one password is the single most exploited weakness in personal security. A password manager fixes it for the price of a coffee a month.

What to look for

  • End-to-end encryption with a master password only you know. Nothing else is acceptable.
  • Audited apps and a clear security whitepaper — not just "military-grade" marketing.
  • Browser and phone support, including autofill that works on mobile.
  • Sharing for family or a small team, if you need it.
  • Export. You must be able to leave with your data.

FAQ

What if I forget the master password? There is no reset — that is the point. Store a recovery copy somewhere physical and safe.

Is a browser manager safe enough? For most people, yes — much safer than reused passwords.
