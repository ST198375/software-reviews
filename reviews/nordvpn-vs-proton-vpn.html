The honest decision rule

  • You travel a lot and stream from many regions → NordVPN's bigger network usually wins.
  • You want the strongest privacy posture and open, auditable apps → Proton VPN.
  • You want to test before paying → Proton VPN free tier is the only real option here.
Both are reputable. Choosing "wrong" between these two costs you less than choosing a cheap unknown VPN that logs everything and sells it.
