WPS Office vs LibreOffice: which free office suite wins

Both are free, both open Microsoft files, and they fail in different places. Here is the honest split.

Ads / upsellpresent in the free tiernone

Best forpeople who exchange files with Microsoft users dailypeople who value zero prompts and full offline control

Decision rule

  • You email .docx files back and forth all day → WPS Office — fewer surprises for the recipient.
  • You work offline on your own documents and hate interruptions → LibreOffice.
  • You need light PDF handling → WPS Office.

Our pick for most people: WPS Office — it loses the least when a document leaves your machine.

FAQ
