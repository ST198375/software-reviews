What actually matters

  • Restore time. Test it once while nothing is broken.
  • Bootable rescue media. If the system disk dies, you need to boot from USB — create that USB today, not later.
  • Second copy off the machine. An image on the same failing disk protects nothing.

Our pick for most homes: AOMEI Backupper — a free tier that covers the essentials and a restore process without drama.
