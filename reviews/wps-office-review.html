WPS Office review: the closest thing to a free Microsoft Office

WPS Office opens .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files, looks familiar from the first minute, and has a free tier that covers everyday documents. The interesting question is where the free tier stops.

What works well

  • Format compatibility — the hard part of replacing Office, and WPS handles it well, including tables and most formulas.
  • Offline desktop apps — documents on your machine, not only in a browser tab.
  • PDF tools built in — export and basic handling without another app.
  • Smaller and lighter than the full Microsoft suite.

Where the free tier ends
