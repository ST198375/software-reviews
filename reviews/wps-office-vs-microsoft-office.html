Switching away from Microsoft Office raises one fear: that your documents will look wrong on someone else's screen. Here is where that fear is justified and where it is not.

When switching is safe

  • You write documents, build simple spreadsheets and make slides.
  • You exchange files with people who use Microsoft Office, and your documents are not formula-heavy.
  • You work on your own machine and do not rely on real-time co-editing.

When it is not

  • Macro-driven workbooks. VBA behaviour differs and business-critical sheets can silently break. Test before migrating.
  • Teams working in one document simultaneously. Microsoft's co-authoring is still ahead.
  • Corporate templates with strict formatting. Pagination can shift.

Our verdict: for individual and small-office use, WPS Office covers the job at a fraction of the cost. Keep the test simple — open your most complex real document and check the pagination.

FAQ

Can both be installed together? Yes, but file associations get confusing — pick one as the default for .docx.

Will recipients see a difference? On everyday documents, no. On complex ones, always check before sending.
