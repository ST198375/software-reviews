AdGuard review: block ads everywhere, not just in one browser

Browser extensions stop ads in a browser. Roughly half of the ads you meet are not in a browser at all. That is the gap AdGuard closes.

What it does differently

  • System-wide blocking on desktop: ads and trackers are filtered before the page or the app sees them.
  • App filtering — including in applications that ignore browser extensions.
  • DNS-level protection for phones and routers, so every device on the network gets it.
  • Filters you can inspect — the rules are open, which matters if you care where the politics of a blocklist sit.
