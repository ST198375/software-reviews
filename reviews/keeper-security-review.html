Keeper Security review: password manager for families and teams

Keeper sits in the same category as the big password managers. The question is what it does better — and whether you already have one included in something else you pay for.

What it does well

  • Strong encryption model with a zero-knowledge architecture and published security documentation — that matters more than feature lists here.
  • Family plan with separate vaults. Each member has their own space, not one shared list.
  • Sharing with permissions. You can share a password without revealing it.
  • Desktop, browser and mobile apps with autofill that works in practice, not just on paper.
  • Secure file storage for passports, insurance and documents you need on the road.

Whichever manager you choose: export your passwords from the browser, then delete them there. Two half-used lists is worse than one good one.

FAQ

What if I forget the master password? There is no reset by design. Store a recovery copy physically, somewhere fireproof.

Is a cloud password manager safe? With zero-knowledge encryption, the provider stores ciphertext it cannot read. Verify that claim in their documentation, not in their marketing.
