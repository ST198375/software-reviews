Notta review: turning meetings and lectures into usable text

Notta is an AI transcription and meeting-notes tool. The question that matters is not "does it transcribe" — most do — but whether the output saves you time or creates a new chore.

What it does

  • Transcribes recorded audio and video, with speaker separation on longer recordings.
  • Summarises long recordings into structured notes you can edit.
  • Exports to text, SRT/VTT subtitles and document formats.
  • Works in the browser and in mobile apps — practical for interviews recorded on a phone.

Where it fits

Use caseValue
Meeting notesHigh — you stop taking minutes by hand
Interviews and researchHigh — searchable text of every conversation
Subtitles for videoMedium — still needs a careful read before publishing
Legal or medical transcriptsLow — treat output as a draft and verify quotes

Our pick: Notta, for long recordings with several speakers and a clean export to subtitles or documents. Start on the free tier and test accuracy on your own audio.

How to test it in 15 minutes

  • Upload five minutes you know well — two speakers, one interruption, some technical terms.
  • Check names and jargon: that is where automatic speech recognition breaks.
  • Decide honestly: is fixing the transcript faster than writing a summary yourself?

FAQ

Does it work offline? No — transcription happens in the cloud, so check your privacy requirements first.

Can it translate? Yes, after transcribing. Quality drops, but summaries across languages are usable.
