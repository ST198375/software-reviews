How to block ads on a phone without root

Ads on mobile are worse than on desktop: they appear in apps, in games and inside browsers you do not control. Here is what actually works, by platform.

What works where

PlatformMethodEffect
AndroidPrivate DNS (system setting)Blocks ads and trackers device-wide, no install
AndroidLocal VPN-based ad blocker (AdGuard)Blocks more, including in-app ads
iOSSafari content blockersBlocks in the browser only, quality varies
iOSDNS profileDevice-wide, but no per-app rules

The simple path most people should take

  • Set a filtering DNS in system settings if your phone allows it — zero install, immediate effect.
  • If you want in-app blocking and per-app rules, install a filter that runs locally.
  • Keep the pause button handy: aggressive filtering breaks some checkouts and login flows.

Our pick for whole-device blocking: AdGuard — DNS filtering plus an app that filters traffic on the device, without root and without sending your traffic through someone's server.

Two myths. First, "ads pay for everything, blocking is theft": many publishers offer paid plans — pay for the ones you read daily. Second, "ad blockers slow the phone": good filtering makes pages faster by removing what slows them down.

FAQ

Will banking apps break? Occasionally — some check for VPN-like connections. Use split tunnelling or pause filtering for that app.

Does DNS filtering work on mobile data? Yes, if set in system settings.
