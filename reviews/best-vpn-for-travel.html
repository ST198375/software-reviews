Best VPN for travel: what matters on hotel and airport Wi-Fi

Travelling changes what you need from a VPN: it is no longer about streaming, it is about hostile networks and unreliable connections.

What matters on the road

  • Automatic protection on unknown Wi-Fi. You will not remember to connect manually.
  • Servers in the country you are visiting — local banking apps often block foreign IPs.
  • Reconnects that survive sleep. Laptops and phones suspend aggressively; a VPN that drops silently leaves you exposed.
  • Multi-device support. Laptop plus phone, at least.
  • Kill switch. Non-negotiable on public networks.

Configuration that actually protects you

FAQ
