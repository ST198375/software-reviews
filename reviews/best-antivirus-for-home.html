Best antivirus for home use: protection vs. performance

Modern Windows is decent on its own. A paid suite earns its money on three things: phishing protection, ransomware rollback and a firewall you can actually control.

What separates a good suite

  • Real-time protection with measurable impact. Time to boot and file copy speed matter more than any lab score.
  • Phishing protection that works in the browser, not only on file downloads.
  • Ransomware rollback — the ability to restore files that were encrypted before detection.
  • Password manager and VPN included — often cheaper than buying each separately.
  • Renewal terms. Security software is where renewal prices jump the most.

FAQ

Can two antivirus programs run together? No — they conflict and can leave you less protected than one good suite.

Is free antivirus enough? For careful users, often yes. The paid extras are phishing protection and rollback.
