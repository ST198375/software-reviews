Proton VPN review: privacy-first, and it shows in the details

Proton VPN is built by the company behind Proton Mail — a Swiss outfit whose entire business model is paid privacy rather than advertising. That single fact explains most of its strengths and one of its limits.

What you get

  • Swiss jurisdiction — among the strongest privacy protections in the world, and outside the usual intelligence-sharing agreements.
  • Audited no-logs policy — an independent audit is evidence, unlike the claim itself.
  • WireGuard by default. Modern protocol means less speed loss than older VPNs.
  • A free tier that is not a demo: unlimited data with fewer servers. Realistically useful.
  • Secure Core — traffic routed through hardened datacentres in privacy-friendly countries, at the cost of some speed.

FAQ

Is the free version a trap? No, but it is limited: fewer countries, no Secure Core, and you are one of many on those servers.

Do I need the bundle with Mail and Drive? Only if you want the whole Proton ecosystem — the VPN alone is the part most people need.
