Best free office suite: when you do not want a subscription

If all you need is documents, spreadsheets and slides, you do not have to rent software forever. Here is what to use instead.

What to look for

  • File compatibility with .docx, .xlsx and .pptx — including complex formatting and formulas.
  • Offline work. Cloud-only editors fail exactly when you need them.
  • PDF handling. Export and light editing save installing another app.
  • Licence terms. Free for personal use is not the same as free for a business.
