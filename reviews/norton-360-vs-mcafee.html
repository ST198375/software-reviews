Norton 360 vs McAfee: which security bundle to buy

Both sell a bundle rather than a plain antivirus. The difference is what you actually get for the money, and how painful the renewal is.

Decision rule

  • You want backup plus protection → Norton 360.
  • You care about identity and dark-web monitoring → McAfee's higher tiers.
  • You only need antivirus → neither bundle; buy a basic tier and save the difference.

Our pick for households: Norton 360 — the bundle covers antivirus, password manager and a light VPN in one subscription.

Both vendors pre-install trial versions on many new laptops. Uninstall the one you do not plan to pay for — two suites on one machine protect you worse than one.

FAQ

Are the bundles worth it if I already have a VPN? Then you are paying twice for one feature — compare the standalone antivirus tiers instead.

Can I move the licence to a new laptop? Yes, after uninstalling from the old device.
