Best AI transcription tools: meetings, interviews and lectures

Transcription stopped being a typing job. The question now is which tool turns a recording into text you can actually use.

What matters in practice

  • Language coverage and accents. English with heavy accents is the usual failure case — test it on your own audio.
  • Speaker separation. Without it, a two-hour meeting transcript is unreadable.
  • Editing and export. SRT/VTT for video, DOCX for notes, plain text for search.
  • Minutes included and overage pricing. This is where costs surprise people.
  • Privacy. Decide whether recordings of your meetings may be uploaded at all.

How to test any transcription tool in 15 minutes

  • Upload a five-minute recording you know well — with two speakers, one interrupting.
  • Check names and technical terms: that is where automatic speech recognition breaks.
  • Check whether paragraphs and speaker turns come out usable or need manual fixing.
  • Compare the effort to fix it against simply typing a summary yourself.
For interviews and meetings, always keep the original audio. Transcripts are a convenience layer; the recording is the evidence.

FAQ

Is AI transcription accurate enough for legal work? Not on its own — treat output as a draft and verify quotations against the audio.

Can it translate? Many tools translate after transcribing; quality drops, but summaries across languages work well.
