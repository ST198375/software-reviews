Best screen recorders

Screen recording is one of the few jobs where the free tool is genuinely the professional one. It is also a job where the paid tools win on everything except recording.

Free path

OBS records your screen with no watermark, no time limit and no account, and it is the same software that streams live events. The cost is a settings screen that assumes you know what a bitrate is. Set the output to a plain MP4, pick the monitor, check the microphone level once — after that it behaves. For anything you will record more than twice, this is the sensible choice.

Quick path

Windows can record a window with the built-in capture tool, which is enough for a bug report or a two-minute demo. No editing, no captions, and audio handling is the weak spot.

Team path

Loom's trick is not recording — it is the instant link, the viewer reactions and the automatic transcript. If your video is a message rather than a product, that convenience is worth the subscription. Remember what you are trading: the file sits on someone else's server, and the free tier limits both duration and how many videos you keep.

Recording and editing in one place

DemoCreator bundles capture with an editor close to Filmora's, which suits tutorials, courses and narrated demos where a recording turns into a finished lesson. If you already pay for an editor with a decent capture module, paying twice makes little sense — check what you own first.

Verdict: start with OBS if you can spend an hour learning it, and with Loom if the video is a message to a human. Pay for a recorder when you also need the editor that comes with it.

Whichever tool you pick, record thirty seconds and watch it back before the real session. Half of all ruined recordings are a muted microphone or the wrong monitor, and both show up in the first ten seconds of a test file.

FAQ

Do free screen recorders add watermarks? OBS and the Windows built-in tool do not. Many free browser recorders do, or cap you at a few minutes.

Can I record a browser tab with system audio? Yes, but audio routing is the part that breaks most often. Test it before recording something you cannot repeat.

Is recording a meeting legal? Depends on where you and the other participants are. Ask first — a recorded meeting is personal data.
