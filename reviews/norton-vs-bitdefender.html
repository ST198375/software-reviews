Norton vs Bitdefender: which antivirus is worth your money

Two names dominate home security. They are aimed at different buyers, and picking the wrong one costs you either money or features.

Decision rule

  • You want to stop paying for a separate password manager and a light VPN → Norton 360.
  • You already have those tools and only need protection → Bitdefender, usually cheaper.
  • You are on a small laptop where every percent of speed counts → test both trial versions and compare boot time yourself.

Our pick for most households: Norton 360 — one subscription replaces two or three.
